Shrimp Green

Neocaridina davidi var. 'Green' (Green Jade/Emerald)
Image is for illustrative purposes only and may not represent all individuals of this species.
Temperament
Peaceful
Grows To
1-1/2"
Starting Tank Size
1 Gallon
Typical Color
Bold emerald to jade green, standout against dark substrate
CB's Recommended Fish Food
Liquid Garden Shrimp Pellets
Sear Shrimp Granules
Care Notes
Undemanding, fast breeder, great starter shrimp; heater generally not required in a stable room; keep groups of 3-4 minimum (shrimp are social, singles hide); avoid ammonia, nitrite, and nitrate over 20 ppm; keep isolated from other Neocaridina colors.
Temp Range
64-84°F
Water PH
6.5-8.0
Water KH
2-4 dKH
Origin
Taiwan
Plant Safety
Safe

Shrimp Orange

Neocaridina davidi var. 'Orange' (Orange Sakura)
Image is for illustrative purposes only and may not represent all individuals of this species.
Temperament
Peaceful
Grows To
1"
Starting Tank Size
1 Gallon
Typical Color
Warm tangerine to deep pumpkin-orange, opaque on higher grades
CB's Recommended Fish Food
Liquid Garden Shrimp Pellets
Sear Shrimp Granules
Care Notes
Care as easy as Cherry shrimp; some keepers report this color line is a bit more sensitive to parameter swings than other Neocaridina, so keep changes gradual; weekly partial water changes to hold nitrates down; keep separate from other colors to maintain the orange line.
Temp Range
68-82°F
Water PH
6.5-7.8
Water KH
3-10 dKH
Origin
China, Taiwan, Vietnam (wild color form, line developed in Japan)
Plant Safety
Safe

Shrimp Ocean Blue

Neocaridina davidi
Image is for illustrative purposes only and may not represent all individuals of this species.
Temperament
Peaceful
Grows To
1-1/2"
Starting Tank Size
1 Gallon
Typical Color
Deep, solid ocean-blue coverage extending into legs and rostrum
CB's Recommended Fish Food
Liquid Garden Shrimp Pellets
Sear Shrimp Granules
Care Notes
Hardy Neocaridina, does well straight from dechlorinated tap water; easy for beginners; keep only one Neocaridina color line per tank or offspring revert to brown/wild color; peaceful scavenger, safe in groups.
Temp Range
68-82°F
Water PH
6.5-7.8
Water KH
2-5 dKH
Origin
Taiwan (selectively bred color line)
Plant Safety
Safe

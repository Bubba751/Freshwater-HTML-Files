Shrimp Orange Rili

Neocaridina davidi var. 'Orange Rili'
Image is for illustrative purposes only and may not represent all individuals of this species.
Temperament
Peaceful
Grows To
1-1/2"
Starting Tank Size
1 Gallon
Typical Color
Bicolor pattern - solid orange head and tail with a clear/translucent band across the midsection
CB's Recommended Fish Food
Liquid Garden Shrimp Pellets
Sear Shrimp Granules
Care Notes
Same easy care as other Neocaridina; best rili pattern and color intensity shown in harder water (GH 8+); avoid sudden temperature or pH swings despite general hardiness; keep isolated from other color lines to preserve the rili pattern in offspring.
Temp Range
68-78°F
Water PH
6.5-7.6
Water KH
3-10 dKH
Origin
Taiwan (selectively bred from Orange Sakura/Pumpkin line)
Plant Safety

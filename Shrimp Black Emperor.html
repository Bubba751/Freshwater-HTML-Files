Shrimp Black Emperor

Caridina cantonensis (Taiwan Bee line)
Image is for illustrative purposes only and may not represent all individuals of this species.
Temperament
Peaceful
Grows To
1-1/2"
Starting Tank Size
1 Gallon
Typical Color
Solid deep black body with crisp white/pale banding
CB's Recommended Fish Food
Liquid Garden Shrimp Pellets
Sear Shrimp Granules
Care Notes
Advanced-level Caridina; needs soft, acidic, very stable water, ideally RO/DI remineralized with a Caridina-specific mineralizer; sensitive to swings in pH/TDS and to copper; needs mature tank with established biofilm; do not house with Neocaridina (interbreeding reverts offspring to wild color).
Temp Range
70-76°F
Water PH
6.0-6.8
Water KH
0-2 dKH
Origin
Taiwan / China (selectively bred)

Safe

Shrimp Amano

Caridina multidentata (syn. C. japonica)
Image is for illustrative purposes only and may not represent all individuals of this species.
Temperament
Peaceful
Grows To
3"
Starting Tank Size
1 Gallon
Typical Color
Translucent silver-blue/tan body with dark red-brown speckles and a broken stripe down the back
CB's Recommended Fish Food
Liquid Garden Shrimp Pellets
Sear Shrimp Granules
Care Notes
Best algae eater in the hobby (hair, thread, and beard algae); very hardy and tolerant of a wide range of parameters; totally peaceful, safe with other dwarf shrimp; will NOT breed in a freshwater tank - larvae require brackish water; keep in groups of 6+; avoid copper-based medications.
Temp Range
65-80°F
Water PH
6.0-7.6
Water KH
2-8 dKH
Origin
Japan, Taiwan, Korea
Plant Safety
Safe

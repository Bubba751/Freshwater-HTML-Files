Shrimp Bloody Mary

Neocaridina davidi var. 'Bloody Mary'
Image is for illustrative purposes only and may not represent all individuals of this species.
Temperament
Peaceful
Grows To
1-1/2"
Starting Tank Size
1 Gallon
Typical Color
Deep, glowing crimson-red - color comes from pigment in the flesh/tissue rather than the shell, giving a translucent 'lit from within' look
CB's Recommended Fish Food
Liquid Garden Shrimp Pellets
Sear Shrimp Granules
Care Notes
Easy care, same base requirements as Cherry shrimp; color intensifies in harder water (GH 8+); keep isolated from other Neocaridina lines to prevent crossbreeding back to wild brown; social, keep in colonies of 5-10+.
Temp Range
68-82°F
Water PH
6.5-7.5
Water KH
2-8 dKH
Origin
Taiwan (bred from the Chocolate shrimp line)
Plant Safety
Safe
